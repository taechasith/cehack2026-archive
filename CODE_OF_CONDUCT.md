# Community Code of Conduct

This archive exists to credit and preserve the work of young creators. Participation in its issues, pull requests, and discussions must be respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Credit creators accurately.
- Give specific, useful feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not expose personal contact information.
- Accept good-faith corrections from project authors.
- Keep technical and editorial discussion focused on the archive.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized content directed at participants, deliberate misattribution, and publication of private information are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the archive. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their verified public contact channel. Do not publish sensitive evidence in a public issue.
